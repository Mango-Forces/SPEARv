Private science runtime notices

The private runtime keeps the complete license and notice files supplied by
each pinned wheel. They are not relabeled as the workspace license.
notices.json records their exact identities.

- CPython includes the PSF license and bundled component notices.
- NumPy includes BSD and bundled OpenBLAS/LAPACK/GCC-runtime notices.
- Astropy includes its top-level license and the WCSLIB LGPL v3 notice;
  LGPL source and relinking obligations need release review.
- ERFA, PyYAML, packaging and IERS data keep their package-specific notices.

This is a recorded notice inventory, not complete legal clearance. The engine
image that composes this runtime with the Rust binary and CFITSIO adds their
notices.

The GPL Python package named fitsio, Photutils and the unselected reprojection
extras are not installed.

Build the runtime on the host with the shared measurement lock:

  flock /tmp/opencode/spearv-ab-20261113/measurement.lock taskset -c 16-19 \
    python3 spine/runtime/build.py all

Archives are pinned in manifest.json and requirements.lock and land under the
ignored .build/runtime directory; nothing is installed on the host.
